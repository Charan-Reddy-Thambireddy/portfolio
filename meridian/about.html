About this demo

Meridian is a portfolio sample of a subscription market-intelligence platform: a research taxonomy, market sizes and forecasts, analyst reports, search, a source-cited assistant and per-subscription access control.

Things to try

  1. In Explore categories, choose Soft Drinks, then India and China, and select View data. Compare the chart and table, then export the data as CSV.
  2. Add Germany. You will see an access notice, because Asha's subscription covers Drinks in Asia Pacific only. Switch to Mei Lin (Global) in the bar at the top, and Germany appears.
  3. Search for cola India: "cola" maps to the Carbonates category through a synonym list, and the filters narrow results by type, industry and country.
  4. In Ask Meridian, ask "Which company leads energy drinks in Indonesia?" and follow the citations to the sources.

How the full application is designed

This demo runs entirely in the browser so it can be hosted as a static site. The production design it stands in for:

  • Front end: Angular micro-UIs (search, explorer, dashboards, assistant), with RxJS/NgRx state and lazy-loaded modules.
  • API: ASP.NET Core (.NET 8) services for market data, reports, search, companies, exports and the assistant, behind Azure AD (OAuth 2.0 / OIDC) sign-in.
  • Entitlements: an authorization policy that checks every request against the organisation's subscription (industries × countries) before data leaves the API.
  • Data: SQL Server for the market time series and taxonomy (columnstore and covering indexes for wide year ranges), Cosmos DB for report metadata, Redis for hot query results.
  • Events: a Kafka "data release" topic; consumers rebuild the search index and evict cached results when analysts publish new figures.
  • Assistant: intent extraction, retrieval over the search index, entitlement filtering, then an LLM writes the answer from the retrieved passages only, with citations.

Accessibility

Built to WCAG 2.1 AA: semantic HTML, keyboard-operable tree and filters, an error summary that takes focus, live-region status messages, charts with marker shapes and a data table, and layouts that reflow down to 320 pixels wide.

Meridian is an independent portfolio demonstration by Charan Reddy. All companies, figures, forecasts and reports are fictional and generated in the browser; country names are real. It is not affiliated with any market research company.
